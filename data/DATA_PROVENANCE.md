# TrustTrace AI — Data Provenance & Governance Specification

## 1. Overview
In compliance with the TrustTrace AI charter, all data entering the training, verification, and inference pipelines must maintain unambiguous provenance. At no point is synthetic data masqueraded as genuine field observations, nor are missing targets inferred as absolute ground truth.

## 3. Strict Provenance Flagging

Every single record in the database and CSV pipeline carries the mandatory `data_provenance` attribute:
- `real_curated`: Derived from documented public supply chain / clinical benchmark datasets with genuine historical distributions.
- `synthetic_simulated`: Generated by the deterministic physics-based simulator modeling transport transit times, customs clearance bottlenecks, sensor gaps, and product-specific thermal degradation curves according to WHO and FDA Title 21 CFR guidelines.

- **Total Usable Records:** 16,000

## 4. Temperature Threshold Policy by Product Category

Temperature thresholds are configured based on international cold-chain standards:

| Category | Target Min (°C) | Target Max (°C) | Allowed Excursion Buffer | Consequence of Critical Breach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vaccines & Biologics** | +2.0°C | +8.0°C | 30 minutes (> +12°C or < 0°C) | Immediate quarantine; potency loss |
| **Ultra-Cold Biopharma** | -25.0°C | -15.0°C | 15 minutes (> -10°C) | Irreversible degradation |
| **Fresh Produce** | +0.0°C | +4.0°C | 4 hours (> +8°C or < -1°C) | Accelerated decay, microbial spoilage |
| **Frozen Food** | -22.0°C | -18.0°C | 2 hours (> -12°C) | Thaw-refreeze crystallization |
| **Ambient Pharmaceuticals** | +15.0°C | +25.0°C | 12 hours (> +30°C or < +10°C) | Chemical instability |
| **Industrial / Electronics** | +5.0°C | +35.0°C | 24 hours (> +45°C or < -5°C) | Condensation / solder stress |

## 5. Distinction Between Observed Facts and Model Inference

1. **Missing Sensor Readings**: A gap where IoT logger transmissions failed. Does not imply an excursion.
2. **Normal Temperature Variation**: Minor fluctuations within approved safe limits.
3. **Temperature Excursion**: Documented sensor reading outside approved limits.
4. **Potential Quality Risk**: AI-inferred probability of batch degradation.
5. **Confirmed Product Damage**: Requires independent physical inspection and lab assay evidence.
